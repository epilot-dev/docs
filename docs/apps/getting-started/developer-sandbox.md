# Developer Sandbox

A sandbox is a separate epilot organization for building and testing. It behaves like a production organization, but contains no live customer data, so a mistake in your App or its configuration affects nobody.

Build your App in a sandbox if you:

- are developing an App for the Marketplace and have no epilot organization of your own, or
- want to try an App out before installing it in your production organization.

## Getting a sandbox

1. **Request a sandbox** through the [developer portal](https://portal.dev.epilot.cloud/request-sandbox).
2. **Sign in** with the credentials you receive once the request is approved.
3. **Create your App**: follow the [quick start](/docs/apps/getting-started/quick-start) or open the [app creation page](https://portal.epilot.cloud/app/apps/configuration/new).

## From sandbox to production

An App you create is private. What happens next depends on who the App is for:

- **For your own company**: a private App can be installed in the organizations [connected to its owner](/docs/apps/getting-started/private-vs-public#private-apps-in-connected-organizations). An App built in a sandbox can therefore be installed in the production organization the sandbox belongs to, without publishing it.
- **For other epilot customers**: submit a version for [review](/docs/apps/publishing/verification-process). Once approved, it is public and available in the Marketplace.
